# SignalIQ System Architecture

## Purpose

SignalIQ is a threat intelligence platform designed to help security analysts reduce cognitive overload by automatically collecting, enriching, organizing, and eventually prioritizing cyber threat intelligence from multiple public sources.

Rather than replacing analyst expertise, SignalIQ automates repetitive data collection and organization so analysts can spend more time investigating threats and making informed security decisions.

Modern security analysts rarely struggle because they lack threat intelligence. They struggle because threat intelligence is fragmented across dozens of disconnected platforms, each presenting information differently.

Investigating a single threat often requires switching between vulnerability databases, threat intelligence feeds, vendor advisories, MITRE ATT&CK documentation, security blogs, and internal telemetry before meaningful analysis can even begin.

SignalIQ is designed to reduce analyst cognitive overload by bringing that information together into a single workflow.

---

## Problem Statement

Modern security teams consume intelligence from many disconnected sources, including CISA advisories, the National Vulnerability Database (NVD), vendor advisories, MITRE ATT&CK, threat intelligence feeds, and internal security tools.

Although each source provides valuable information, analysts often have to manually correlate vulnerabilities, threat context, attacker techniques, and remediation guidance before they can begin investigating.

As the volume of cyber threat intelligence continues to grow, analysts spend increasing amounts of time gathering and organizing information instead of responding to threats.

The result is slower investigations, inconsistent prioritization, and increased cognitive fatigue.

---

## Project Vision

SignalIQ is designed to transform fragmented threat intelligence into contextual, prioritized, and actionable insights through automation.

The long-term vision is to help analysts spend less time searching for information and more time investigating and responding to threats.

---

## Research Question

How can security teams reduce analyst cognitive overload by transforming fragmented threat intelligence into contextual, prioritized, and actionable intelligence?
